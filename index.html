Clueban Privacy Policy

Effective date and last updated: 12 November 2026

Emanuel Eduardo ("we", "us", or "our") develops Clueban for iOS and macOS. This policy explains how Clueban handles your information and how we handle privacy and support messages.

Privacy contact: eeduardofr@gmail.com.

Clueban does not require a Clueban account. Your library content is stored on your device or in folders you choose. Clueban does not automatically send that content to us, and we do not operate a server that hosts your libraries.

1. Information Clueban stores

Clueban stores the information you create or import, including projects, boards, columns, cards, notes, comments, subtasks, owner text, tags, relationships, templates, and layouts. Records can include identifiers, creation and modification dates, due dates, completion dates, and archive status. This information supports the app's editing, search, organization, and export features.

The app also stores device preferences, onboarding status, selected folder locations, and system access bookmarks. Local recovery files can contain pending card text and other unsaved changes. Cabinet folders contain metadata and recovery history that can include earlier versions of your content.

When you import an ordinary Markdown folder, Clueban preserves a copy of the complete source tree in the destination cabinet's Imported Originals folder. This includes attachments and hidden files. Import and migration operations preserve their source files.

2. Cloud storage and shared folders

You can choose a local folder, iCloud Drive, or another storage provider available through the system folder picker. If you choose a cloud folder, that provider can upload and sync the cabinet's files, including content, metadata, imported originals, and recovery history. The provider handles these transfers through your account. Clueban does not receive your storage account password.

The original Clueban database remains local. The current app uses folder storage for cross-device access and does not sync that database through a private CloudKit service.

If you share a cabinet folder, people who can access it may also access its contents and history. Your provider's terms, privacy policy, sharing settings, retention rules, and international transfer practices apply. For iCloud Drive, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Apple Reminders

When you choose Add to Reminders, Clueban asks for Reminders permission. Apple's permission is full access to Reminders. Clueban uses it to create a reminder in your default writable list. The reminder contains the card title, full notes, an optional due date, and the text #Clueban. The copy can include edits currently shown in the card editor that you have not saved to the cabinet.

Clueban does not fetch your existing reminders or calendar events. The new reminder is an independent copy. Later changes and deletion do not sync between the card and reminder.

The account for your Reminders list may sync the copy through iCloud or another service. You can revoke Clueban's Reminders access in system privacy settings. This does not delete reminders already created. Delete those copies in Reminders.

4. Imports, exports, and other copies

Imports are processed on your device. Clueban does not sign in to Trello or another service to retrieve an imported database; you select an existing file.

JSON exports include your library content and metadata, including archived items. You choose where to save or share them. A cloud destination or recipient can retain a separate copy. Deleting or changing the original cabinet does not change an export, import source, migrated copy, or recipient's copy.

5. Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

Clueban has no advertising, analytics, third-party tracking SDKs, or app-integrated crash reporting service. We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Clueban does not use your content for advertising or profiling.

Apple may separately process App Store, purchase, diagnostic, or service information under its own privacy policy and your system settings. If you choose to send us feedback, screenshots, or diagnostic details, we handle that information as described below.

The public Privacy Policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it in your browser sends GitHub standard web request information, such as your IP address, browser details, and request time. GitHub handles hosting logs under its Privacy Statement. The page has no scripts, cookies, external fonts, or analytics added by us. The app does not attach cabinet content or identifiers to the link.

6. Information you send us

If you contact us, we receive your email address, any name you provide, your message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you send. Providing this information is optional. Please include only the information needed for your request.

We use it to answer your request, investigate the issue you report, and meet applicable legal obligations. Emanuel Eduardo is the data controller for this correspondence. Where the GDPR or similar laws apply, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to requests and maintaining Clueban, or on a legal obligation where applicable. We do not use support messages for advertising.

Our contact address uses Google Gmail to deliver and store correspondence. Google may process it outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and its data transfer safeguards. We may disclose information we hold if required by law.

We keep correspondence only as long as needed to resolve the request and any related issue, or to meet applicable legal retention requirements. You can request deletion using the privacy contact above.

7. Retention and deletion

Your library stays in the storage location you choose until you remove it. Clueban does not apply an automatic expiration period. Archiving keeps content. Editing or deleting an item does not necessarily remove earlier text from cabinet history, conflict versions, imported originals, or backups. Cabinet recovery history is not automatically pruned.

To remove a folder cabinet, stop using it in Clueban and remove the complete cabinet folder through Files, Finder, or your storage provider, including hidden history. Do not remove individual history files from a cabinet you still use; the app needs them for recovery. Manage cloud trash, prior versions, backups, exported files, migration sources, and Reminders copies separately through the relevant service.

Uninstalling Clueban does not delete user-selected folders or those separate copies. Local databases, preferences, saved folder access, and recovery files can also remain on the device, particularly on macOS. Contact us for device-specific removal guidance.

We cannot remotely access or delete content stored only on your device or in your chosen storage account.

8. Security

Clueban uses system file access permissions. Folder cabinets use readable files and do not have separate Clueban encryption or password protection. Their protection depends on your device security, file permissions, and storage provider. Use access controls appropriate for the information you store. No storage or transmission method can guarantee complete security.

9. Your privacy rights

You can view, edit, and export locally available content in Clueban. You control your cabinet files and storage provider settings.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information we hold, and to restrict or object to its processing. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact eeduardofr@gmail.com to make a request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not use personal information to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

10. Children

Clueban is a general-purpose productivity app and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support correspondence. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us to request its removal.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when Clueban or our privacy practices change. We will revise the date at the top and provide any additional notice required by applicable law.

For privacy questions, contact Emanuel Eduardo at eeduardofr@gmail.com.
